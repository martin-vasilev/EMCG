Eye-Movement Control Group

Eye movements are one of the fastest motor responses in humans. We study how people control the movements of their eyes during everyday tasks such as reading, scanning and scene viewing. Our work combines eye tracking, computational modelling and methodological advancements to reveal the dynamics of human attention.

Attention & distraction
