Eye-Movement Control Group

Eye movements are one of the fastest motor responses in humans. We study how people control the movements of their eyes during everyday tasks such as reading, scanning and scene viewing. Our work combines eye tracking, computational modelling and methodological advancements to reveal the dynamics of human attention. We are the only group at UCL conducting fundamental research into eye-movements.

Research themes

Learn more

Attention & distraction

We study how auditory distractors affect visual attention and eye-movement control.

Eye movement control in reading

We study how readers select saccade targets, use parafoveal/ peripheral information and coordinate their eyes during multiline reading.

Computational modelling

We use computational models and machine learning to explain eye-movement behaviour and attentional dynamics, including in neurodevelopmental conditions such as ADHD.
