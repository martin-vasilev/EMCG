Opportunities

Join the group

We welcome enquiries from students and researchers interested in eye movements, attention, reading, distraction, visual cognition, computational modelling and accessible eye tracking. If your interests overlap with our work, please get in touch well before funding deadlines so that there is time to discuss project fit and develop an application.

Doctoral study

PhD funding routes

UCL Research Excellence Scholarship

A university-wide route for outstanding prospective doctoral researchers. It is open to applicants from any country and can cover tuition fees, a maintenance stipend and research costs.

Scheme information ↗

UCL Research Opportunity Scholarship

This scheme supports UK/home-fee applicants from Black, Asian and minority ethnic groups that are under-represented among UCL postgraduate researchers. It combines financial support with a programme of professional development.

Scheme information ↗

UBEL ESRC Doctoral Training Partnership

UBEL provides ESRC-funded doctoral studentships across the social sciences. This can be an appropriate route for projects centred on psychology, behaviour, cognition or related social-science questions, subject to the current pathway and eligibility rules.

UBEL DTP ↗

UCL–Birkbeck MRC Doctoral Training

MRC doctoral funding can be relevant to projects with a strong biomedical or mental-health focus. This is particularly worth considering for work linking eye movements and attentional mechanisms to clinical or neurodevelopmental questions.

Explore MRC doctoral funding ↗

LIDo DTP

LIDo is a four-year multidisciplinary doctoral programme spanning biological, biomedical, computational, engineering and quantitative research. Projects that combine eye tracking with computational or biological questions may fit its remit.

LIDo applications ↗

EPSRC Doctoral Landscape Award

EPSRC funding at UCL supports four-year doctoral projects within engineering and physical-sciences remit, including relevant computational and methodological work. UCL currently runs the EPSRC Doctoral Landscape Award and related project-based routes.

Early-career researchers

Postdoctoral funding routes

British Academy Postdoctoral Fellowship

A major route for early-career researchers in the humanities and social sciences who are developing an independent research programme. Psychology projects can be suitable where their primary contribution falls within the Academy's disciplinary remit.

Scheme information ↗

British Academy International Fellowship

A two-year fellowship for outstanding early-career researchers from outside the UK who want to establish research experience at a UK institution. It can provide a route for an international researcher to join the group with a focused independent project.

Scheme information ↗

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowship

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships support international mobility and researcher development through an individual research project hosted by a participating institution. They are particularly useful for applicants seeking to bring complementary methods or expertise to the group.

MSCA fellowships ↗

Leverhulme Early Career Fellowship

Leverhulme Early Career Fellowships support researchers at an early stage of their academic careers to undertake a significant piece of publishable research. Applications require institutional support and therefore need to be planned well ahead of the funder's deadline.

Scheme information ↗

Wellcome Early-Career Award

Wellcome supports early-career researchers who are ready to develop their own research identity in discovery research relevant to human life, health and wellbeing. It may suit projects where eye-movement science addresses a substantial health or clinical question.

Scheme information ↗

EPSRC Postdoctoral Pathway

UCL's EPSRC doctoral funding portfolio also includes Postdoctoral Pathway Fellowships for eligible researchers emerging from EPSRC-funded doctoral training. These opportunities are call-dependent, so prospective applicants should check the current UCL guidance.

UCL EPSRC opportunities ↗
